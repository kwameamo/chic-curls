Aftercare Tips

💧
Avoid water for 24–48hrsKeep lashes dry after your appointment so the adhesive fully cures.
🚿
No steam or saunasHigh heat and steam loosen the bond and shorten lash retention.
🚫
Don't rub your eyesRubbing or pulling causes damage and premature shedding.
🪮
Brush dailyUse a clean spoolie each morning to keep lashes fluffy and aligned.
🧴
Avoid oil-based productsOils break down the adhesive — check cleansers and makeup removers.
😴
Sleep on your backSide sleeping flattens and bends lashes. A silk pillowcase helps too.

FAQ

A full set takes 1.5 – 2.5 hours depending on the style. Signature sets like Manga or Viper Luxe may take longer due to the precision involved. Plan ahead and arrive relaxed.
With proper aftercare, extensions last 3 – 5 weeks. Natural lashes shed in cycles so your extensions shed with them. We recommend a refill every 2 weeks to keep them looking full.
Not at all — most clients fall asleep! Your eyes stay closed throughout. There may be a very mild sensitivity to adhesive fumes for some people, but no pain is involved.
Come with clean, makeup-free eyes — no mascara, eyeliner, or eye cream. Avoid curling your lashes beforehand. If you wear contacts, bring your glasses as you'll need to remove them.
It's best to skip mascara — your extensions already give you that bold look. If you must, use only water-based mascara on the tips. Waterproof mascara breaks down the adhesive bond.
Classic is one extension per natural lash — natural and defined. Hybrid blends classic and volume for a textured, lush look. Volume uses a fan of ultra-fine lashes per natural lash for maximum fullness and drama.
